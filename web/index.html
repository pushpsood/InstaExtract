Why Traditional Unfollower Apps Get Accounts Banned

If you have ever used an "Unfollowers for Instagram" app on iOS or Android, your account was likely flagged with a suspicious login or suspension. Here is the technical reason why — and how InstaExtract protects you.

🚨

The Datacenter IP Trap

Scam apps run their bots on cloud servers (AWS, DigitalOcean, Hetzner). Meta's edge security immediately flags requests from cloud datacenter ASNs, triggering checkpoint_required locks and temporary account bans.

🏡

The Residential Advantage

InstaExtract runs 100% inside your browser on your laptop. Every request originates from your authentic home residential IP address with your natural browser fingerprint. To Instagram, it looks like normal browsing.

🛡️

Zero Password Storage

We never ask for, transmit, or store your Instagram password. InstaExtract operates using your existing active web session cookies in your browser. You never have to trust an unknown developer with your account credentials.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About InstaExtract

Clear answers to common questions about safety, accuracy, and unfollowing on Instagram.

How do I check who doesn't follow me back on Instagram without giving my password?
InstaExtract runs 100% inside your browser. By using our 1-click Bookmarklet, the script runs in the context of your active, authenticated Instagram tab. Because you are already logged in, the tool reads your followers and following lists directly without ever seeing, asking for, or storing your password.
Why do other Instagram tracker apps get accounts banned or suspended?
Third-party mobile apps ask for your credentials, then query Instagram's servers from cloud server datacenters (like AWS, DigitalOcean, or Hetzner). Meta's edge security monitors IP address ASNs: when traffic arrives from datacenter IPs, it triggers security checkpoints (checkpoint_required), password resets, or account suspensions. InstaExtract runs entirely from your genuine home residential IP with humanized jitter, keeping your account 100% safe.
How does Method B Mobile QR Hand-off work?
Mobile browsers cannot inspect network headers to extract session details. Method B solves this: you run the 60-second extraction safely on your laptop, which generates a dynamic QR code backed by an ephemeral AWS S3 session. When you scan the QR code with your smartphone, the dashboard opens on your phone. Tapping "Open ↗" on any profile card triggers the native Instagram mobile URI scheme (instagram://user?username=...), letting you unfollow accounts with a single thumb tap inside the official Instagram app.
Is InstaExtract really 100% free with no hidden fees?
Yes! InstaExtract is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, no in-app purchases, and no advertisements. You get full access to all follower analytics, CSV exports, and safe unfollow tools.
How long is my data stored in the cloud?
All mobile sync sessions are strictly ephemeral. They are stored in an encrypted AWS S3 bucket configured with an automatic S3 Lifecycle Rule that permanently purges session files after 24 hours. No databases or permanent profile histories are ever created.
What is the difference between Fans, Mutual Friends, and Don't Follow Back?
Don't Follow Back: Accounts you follow who do not follow you back (your prime unfollow candidates).
Fans: Accounts following you that you do not follow back.
Mutual Friends: Reciprocal accounts where both of you follow each other.
Why can't I drag the bookmarklet on Apple Safari?
Apple Safari restricts dragging JavaScript bookmarklets to the bookmarks bar for security reasons. To make auditing effortless on Safari, InstaExtract offers the 1-Click Console Script: simply click "Copy Script", press Cmd + Option + C on Instagram, paste, and press Enter. If you prefer a permanent Safari bookmark, click "View Manual Safari Bookmark Steps" in the Bookmarklet tab to follow our 4-step setup guide.
